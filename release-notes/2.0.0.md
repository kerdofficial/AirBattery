The first release of this fork of [lihaoyun6/AirBattery](https://github.com/lihaoyun6/AirBattery). It is a separate app: it installs next to the original, and settings do not carry over.

## Requirements

- macOS 13 or later

## New

- Logitech devices on Lightspeed, Unifying or Bolt receivers, and Logitech headsets, show their battery level and charging state.
- Bluetooth accessories that macOS reports through system power sources, such as keyboards that were missing before, now appear.
- AirPods show case, left and right levels without opening the case.
- Device icons come from the native macOS device catalog, including widgets.
- A native macOS 26 design: Liquid Glass popover and Dock window, concentric corners, and a native settings window with a sidebar and grouped forms.
- Widgets use the native system background, stay readable when the desktop is dimmed, and reload as soon as device data changes.
- A manual device order, set by dragging in Menu Bar & Dock settings, applied to the popover, Dock, pinned items, widgets and the CLI.
- Sort devices by name or battery level.
- A single "..." menu on each device row replaces the hover buttons.
- Copy a device ID from the device list.
- An option to always show earbuds separately.
- A hidden Developer pane (Option-click the Settings button in the popover) with anonymous local diagnostics you can export and send.
- MacBook Neo is shown with the MacBook icon.

## Improved

- Widgets keep updating while the popover and Dock window are closed, driven by one app-level timer.
- Battery alerts check every device and run from the app timer.
- iPhone and iPad scans are parsed safely, time out cleanly and run as one coalesced scan.
- Pinned menu bar icons refresh when a device type changes.
- Copy across the app is consistent English, with Simplified and Traditional Chinese updated.
- Updated Sparkle, swift-argument-parser and the bundled libimobiledevice tools (OpenSSL 3.5.9).

## Fixed

- A crash after wake and duplicate device rows, by locking the device list and Bluetooth data.
- The Bluetooth log reader works again on macOS 26 and 27.
- AirPods levels are clamped to 0-100, only real Apple advertisements are accepted, and the AirPods 4 ANC icon is correct.
- Bluetooth LE peripherals are released, and ones without a battery service are skipped.
- Crash-prone force casts were removed from the sources.
- The IOService handle is no longer closed on every power read.
- "Never" can be selected for offline device removal.
